From the trail to home.

The ESP32 captures CAN and GPS. The local logger stores it. Home Assistant makes it useful around the house.

Connect to your local logger

The dashboard and API run on the same device. Start the included server, then open this dashboard at its local address.

No bike connection yet. Demo mode is available without hardware.

Phone access: open the logger’s LAN address on your home Wi-Fi. No vendor account or external scripts are needed.

02 / PRESERVE

Locally, first

Append to SD before upload. Batch to the receiver over Wi-Fi and retry until each sequence is acknowledged.

RECEIVER IMPLEMENTED
03 / UNDERSTAND

At home

SQLite history, browser dashboard and optional MQTT sensors for Home Assistant. GPS location stays local.

DASHBOARD + BRIDGE IMPLEMENTED

Home Assistant

Run the included MQTT bridge beside the logger and connect it to your Mosquitto broker. Discovery adds verified sensors for charge, voltage, current, speed and temperature. Provisional readings are excluded by default. The bridge sends fresh readings once per second, with expiry and device availability.

Use Home Assistant for storage reminders and missed-update alerts. Keep high-rate raw captures in the bike’s SD log and the logger database.

Cell detail & energy useThird-party prototype reports; inferencePotentially beyond the OEM UI. Requires validated cell decoding and current sign; usable capacity is not inferred from SOC.

A better log, without assuming an empty bike.

The OEM app advertises ride trajectories, and the T-box describes cloud upload. We cannot establish its retention period, sample rate, export options or on-device log depth from these sources. Bee Link records new data independently rather than relying on undocumented vendor history.

No pin assignment, bitrate, CAN ID or signal scale is supplied as a verified Ultra Bee definition.
